## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (user confirmed: a functional static mockup to open and present). No framework.

## Users

Primary: purchasing managers and nutrition committees at clinics, IPS and hospitals in Bogotá, evaluating an institutional food supplier. They need proof of food safety, clinical-diet coverage, traceability and legal standing before requesting a proposal or a tasting.

Secondary (confirmed present on current site, lower priority): HR/admin managers buying corporate catering and casinos; restaurants buying vacuum-packed portioned proteins.

## Product Purpose

Catering C.H. Pray S.A.S. is a family-run food company in Bogotá supplying hospital diets, corporate meals/snacks/events and vacuum-packed portioned proteins. The site exists to earn a quote request, a tasting, or a plant audit visit from institutional buyers. Success = qualified quote requests (form / multi-step wizard / WhatsApp).

## Positioning

"Cocinamos como en casa. Cumplimos como una institución." Home-style family cooking held to institutional rigor: individual tray labelling (bed, patient, diet, time), thermal chain (>65°C hot / <4°C cold), per-batch traceability, own plant in Bogotá, nutritionist-calibrated menus.

## Operating Context

Buyers review technical sheets, batch control logs and sanitary documentation for committees; request tastings; visit the plant for audits. Contact via form, WhatsApp (312 615 6192), email direccion@chpray.com.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Lines: hospital diets (normal, blanda/semiblanda, hiposódica, hiperproteica, líquida clara/completa); corporate catering (hot lunch on site, self-service casino, sealed snacks, coffee stations); events/asados; vacuum proteins (lomo fino 220g, punta de anca 250g, pechuga 200g, bondiola 200g).
- BPM per Resolución 2674 de 2013; Ley 1581 de 2012 data treatment; electronic invoicing.
- Proposal in < 48 business hours. Mon–Sat. Coverage Bogotá D.C.
- Plant: Cra. 24D #3-27, Bogotá. NIT 901.815.422-7.
- Existing subpages: alimentacion-hospitalaria/, catering-corporativo/, asados-corporativos/, proteinas-al-vacio/, guias/.

## Product Principles

1. Trust is proven, not claimed: show the mechanism (labels, temperatures, documents).
2. Warmth of a family kitchen, discipline of a clinic — both must be visible.
3. The clinical buyer is first; other lines are reachable but secondary.
4. Every path ends in a low-friction request: quote, tasting or visit.
